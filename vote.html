🇺🇸 United States (Federal)

  • ✅ Register to vote.
    • Already registered? Check you're still enrolled.
    • 🌎 If you're a US citizen, you can vote - no matter where you are in the world. Absentee voting is a standardized process. Check the federal absentee voting program for info.
    • 🧑‍⚖️ Check if your state supports felon voting (it varies wildly from place to place).
  • 🛂 Check if your state needs ID to vote in person.
  • ⏰ Check if your state supports early voting - almost certainly they do. Vote by post!
  • 🫣 Your party registration (and the fact that you voted) is public… but who you vote for is always private. (The Address Confidentiality Program may be able to help conceal the first two.)
  • 👥 President, House rep, senator, governor, comptroller… some states even have ballots to directly influence policy. All are important. Check who and what you can vote for in advance.
  • 📰 The Hill is a distraction-free, policy-focused source.
